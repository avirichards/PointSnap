# PointSnap airline access status

Updated September 6, 2026 UTC. This report separates working PointSnap connections, native browser evidence, cached discovery data and documented access requirements. **All-airline native live search is not complete.** The current user-approved sequence is coverage first: establish native connections with real data/UI checks, then complete sustained hosting and release qualification. American and Etihad are locally integrated; ANA’s current public award entries have been rechecked and reach member login; Southwest and SAS have now passed their initial local integration gates in PointSnap, with earlier unresolved programs still open. Forty-two programs and booking flows are tracked; this is not a claim to have tested every airline worldwide.

## What actually works

PointSnap can search without connecting a traveler's airline account. Thirteen scoped sources now supply individual live flight offers in the configured local app: Alaska Atmos, JetBlue TrueBlue, Aeromexico Rewards, Frontier Miles, Ethiopian ShebaMiles, Skywards' easyJet/Jet2 partner portal, and the anonymous American, Delta, Smiles, Etihad, Southwest, SAS and Copa browser connections. Qantas adds cached international Classic Reward itineraries. Virgin Atlantic supplies a separate daily calendar.

The following are measured searches, not guarantees that every route, date or fare is available. Each source's supplied itineraries, connections and fare choices are retained; individual-source success does not establish complete airline-wide coverage.

## Wider review and remaining options

The approved plan prioritizes direct anonymous connections, then app-operated anonymous browsers, starting with American. Customers must not need airline connections or a helper installation. Operator-held accounts are a fallback for demonstrated member-access needs; an award-data subscription is outside the plan. Optional hosting/compute must fit within the total $100/month infrastructure ceiling. The [access-options assessment](airline-access-options.md) retains earlier alternatives as historical research. The anonymous browser-service option is verified locally for American, Delta and Smiles. The ordinary-Chrome collector now runs independently of the conversation. Member-session recovery remains unverified.
