# Draft: Searching awards with PointSnap

PointSnap searches using its own airline and award-data connections. You do not need to connect a personal airline account.

Choose your departure and destination airports, date, travelers and minimum cabin, then select Find awards. You can explore a route on the map first, but the map is optional and does not show confirmed availability.

Compare each available cabin's points and cash fees. Prices in the table are per person; opening a price shows totals for your party. Daily-calendar results do not name an exact flight. Select your flight on the airline website. Fees shown as unknown must be checked with the airline.

Source coverage distinguishes programs with results, programs checked with no matching awards, and programs that could not be checked. A failed source does not mean an award is unavailable. Searches with a return date check each direction separately as one-way awards.

Use nearby-date buttons to run a fresh search on another day. Save search criteria on this device to revisit them later; saved searches do not monitor prices.

Where PointSnap finds a matching cash fare, it shows USD cents per point after subtracting award fees. The comparison uses the same flights and cabin. Open details to see the cash fare type; its rules may differ from the award. Calendar-only rows and missing fees have no invented comparison.

Your wallet stores balances, expiry dates and card nicknames you enter. You can edit or delete them. Selected award details show whether your saved balance in that program covers your party's points cost. Bank-point transfer ratios and bonuses are not calculated.

Before transferring points, confirm that the airline still offers the exact seats and final price you want.

Daily fare summaries now have their own section below individual flight results. They are leads for choosing a date, not a complete list of departures. JetBlue does not report a cabin; Virgin does report cabins but does not provide individual schedules or exact fees. Open the airline to choose a flight.

Click a cabin price to see all supplied award fare choices for that itinerary, the cabin on each leg, and mixed-cabin warnings. Prices at the top of the comparison table are the lowest supplied fare in each cabin.
